📖 Abbreviations & Key Terms

SUStorage Unit — The unique 7-digit identifier for each drum in SAP (e.g., 6086037)
LT10Transfer Order Creation — SAP transaction used to move materials between storage bins
FIFOFirst In, First Out — The oldest drum in the freezer is the first one removed
BatchA production lot identified by "D" followed by 6 digits (e.g., D796298) — typically 5 drums per batch
LaneOne side of the freezer — 2 lanes per freezer (A and B), separated by a central aisle
PositionA numbered pallet spot — P01 (nearest door) through P11 (back wall)
SlotAn individual drum position on a pallet — 4 slots per pallet (2×2 grid)
F71/72/73Freezer 71, 72, 73 — The three freezers at the Kinsale site

💡 The Analogy — Think of It Like a Car Park

Imagine each freezer is a small car park with 2 rows of 11 spaces.

Each "parking space" is a pallet position. Each pallet holds 4 drums — like 4 motorbikes in one car space. You have 3 car parks (Freezer 71, 72, 73).

The problem before: Every motorbike got the same ticket number regardless of which car park or space it was in. Finding one meant searching all 3 car parks in the cold.

The solution: Now every space has a unique address (e.g., "F71, Lane A, Position 7, Slot 3"). Finding any motorbike is instant — just check the map.

🎨 Batch Colour-Coding

Think of it like colour-coded parking permits.

All drums are the same product, but different batches get different colours. At a glance you can see "all the red drums are Batch D796298, the blue ones are D796315." Click a batch chip above the map to highlight just that batch — everything else dims. Since each batch is 5 drums, you can quickly confirm all 5 are accounted for.

📐 The Naming System

Every drum location follows a simple address:

F71 · Lane A · P07 · Slot 3 → "F71-A-P07-S3"
Reading the address:
F71 = Freezer 71 (or 72 / 73)
A = Lane A or B
P07 = Position 7 (P01 = door on left, P11 = back wall on right)
S3 = Slot 3 on the pallet (1–4 in a 2×2 grid)

📥 How to Place Drums

1
Check the Tracker

The green-highlighted pallet shows the next placement position. System fills from the BACK (P11, right) toward the DOOR (P01, left).

2
Enter SU & select Batch

Type the drum's SU number and pick the batch from the dropdown (or add a new batch). The colour is assigned automatically.

3
Physically place the drum

Take it to the assigned position. The map updates so others can see it.

4
Process LT10 in SAP

Use the new bin format (e.g., F71-A-P07) instead of the generic freezer bin.

Why fill from the back? Like stacking plates — newest at the back, oldest nearest the door. Ensures FIFO and minimises time in the cold.

📤 How to Remove Drums

1
Search for the SU

The map highlights the exact drum with a gold glow.

2
Go directly to it

The address tells you freezer, lane, and position.

3
Remove and log it

Enter the SU in "Remove Drum." Map and log update immediately.

🚨 Capacity Alert System

Think of it like a fuel warning light in a car.

When your petrol tank drops below a quarter, you get an amber light. If you keep going, it turns red. Same idea here — but in reverse, because the freezer is filling up rather than emptying out.

The system monitors all 3 freezers continuously. When capacity reaches a threshold, an alert banner appears on the Tracker tab and an email is automatically prepared to send to the DigiApps team.

🟡 85%WARNING — Capacity threshold reached. Monitor closely and consider redistributing drums across freezers.
🟠 90%HIGH — Approaching full capacity. Plan redistribution or expedite outgoing movements.
🔴 95%CRITICAL — Freezer nearly full. Immediate action required — only a few slots remaining.
How the email works: When a new threshold is crossed, the system automatically opens your email client with a pre-filled message to DigiApps@elililly.onmicrosoft.com. The email includes the freezer name, current fill level, available slots, and a status summary of all 3 freezers. You just hit "Send." Each tier only triggers once per freezer to avoid repeated emails — the alert resets if capacity drops below the threshold.
